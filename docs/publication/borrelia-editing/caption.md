**Assembly editing with matched contact and sequence evidence.** Public
*Borrelia burgdorferi* S9 Hi-C mapped to B31 (GSM7056007; Ren et al., 2023) is
shown before editing (A), after an illustrative cut in Ch1 (B), and after grouping
the children into one scaffold (C). Upper triangles show observed contacts
multiplied by the native row and column Cooler weights, with a common linear
0–0.0612955 scale; excluded balancing bins are masked. Lower triangles show
ModDotPlot estimated sequence identity on the 10 kb grid (k=21, sketch size 1,000,
neighbor expansion 0.5, minimum identity 80%). Blue tracks show GC fraction among
called A/C/G/T bases in 10 kb windows. The orange feature marks an illustrative
cut region. The cut follows source base 450,123; both children retain references
to a crossing source bin. This editing demonstration does not establish a
biological misassembly. Source matrix and FASTA bytes remain unchanged.

**Alt text:** Three panels show the same Borrelia Hi-C map and sequence-identity
layer. A shows the original assembly. In B, an internal boundary splits Ch1 after
base 450,123. In C, its two children belong to one scaffold while retaining their
contig boundary. Gray bands identify contigs and scaffolds. Blue tracks show GC
fraction; an orange feature marks the illustrative cut. A key explains contact
intensity, sequence identity and masking.

Reference: Ren Z, Takacs CN, Brandão HB, Jacobs-Wagner C, Wang X. Organization
and replicon interactions within the highly segmented genome of *Borrelia
burgdorferi*. PLOS Genetics 19:e1010857 (2023).
